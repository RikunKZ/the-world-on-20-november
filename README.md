Entry for make.fun's ["Sum up 20 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120) bounty.

A one-page broadsheet that sums up the world's news of Friday
20 November 2026 in 35 stories across 7 regions: Russian missiles
hitting Kyiv's three Dnipro bridges, Cyclone Akvan's landfall in
Odisha, the AI sell-off that sank Asia's stock markets, the UK-EU
reset deal, COP31's deadlocked last day in Antalya, a Russian
missile in Poland, a tanker ablaze near Hormuz, Italy's general
strike, Japan's inflation, Seoul's impeachment move, Kenya's first
oil well, Bukele's third-term filing and Glenn Phillips's 118 -
plus 22 more stories, a map, and a date check that keeps Thursday
19 November's news (the Shenzhen summit, the Rohingya ruling,
Anthropic's IPO debut) out.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the image the
repository holds to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
