Entry for make.fun's ["Sum up 20 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120) bounty.

One picture that sums up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026:
the Shenzhen three-way summit of Trump, Xi and Putin and their joint
peace declaration; Russian missiles striking Kyiv's Dnipro bridges;
the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya;
Anthropic's $81bn IPO, the largest in history; the US Navy blockade
of Iranian shipping in the Strait of Hormuz; and the COP31 climate
talks in Antalya stalled over fossil fuels.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the image the
repository holds to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
